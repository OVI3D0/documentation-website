# Workloads

A workload defines the benchmarking scenarios that OpenSearch Benchmark runs against your cluster. This section covers how workloads are structured, how to choose and run them, and how to create your own.

- [Anatomy of a workload]({{site.url}}{{site.baseurl}}/benchmark/user-guide/workloads/anatomy-of-a-workload/) --- Understand the structure of a workload: `workload.json`, index templates, operations, test procedures, and data files.
- [Choosing a workload]({{site.url}}{{site.baseurl}}/benchmark/user-guide/workloads/choosing-a-workload/) --- Pick the right workload for your benchmarking needs.
- [Running workloads]({{site.url}}{{site.baseurl}}/benchmark/user-guide/workloads/running-workloads/) --- Execute workloads against your cluster.
- [Creating custom workloads]({{site.url}}{{site.baseurl}}/benchmark/user-guide/workloads/creating-custom-workloads/) --- Build workloads from your own data.
- [Fine-tuning workloads]({{site.url}}{{site.baseurl}}/benchmark/user-guide/workloads/finetune-workloads/) --- Adjust workloads to match production conditions.
- [Contributing workloads]({{site.url}}{{site.baseurl}}/benchmark/user-guide/workloads/contributing-workloads/) --- Share your workloads with the community.

For the full reference on workload components, see [Workload reference]({{site.url}}{{site.baseurl}}/benchmark/reference/workloads/) including [operations]({{site.url}}{{site.baseurl}}/benchmark/reference/workloads/operations/), [parameters]({{site.url}}{{site.baseurl}}/benchmark/reference/workloads/parameters/), and [test procedures]({{site.url}}{{site.baseurl}}/benchmark/reference/workloads/test-procedures/).
